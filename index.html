01 Patterns, not fiction

Most AI assessment tools produce fiction because users would never trust a subscription-based company with the bank of personal information that would make analysis meaningful. We don't face this trade-off because we are a non-profit that gives our tools freely.

02 Rubric-scored

Two practitioners who ask their AI "How effective am I as an AI user?" will receive explanations that mean nothing—separately or together.

Assessment requires a rubric: we measure practice for everyone under identical criteria.

03 Gaps that develop

A named gap is not a verdict—it is a treasure map that shows you where growth can be found.

Shared language enables collective learning, and this is how The Co-Intelligence Readiness Framework turns personal anxiety into collective development.
